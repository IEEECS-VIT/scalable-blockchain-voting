Tally page

Aggregate encrypted ballots, then decrypt through trustee shares.

The version-2 pipeline aggregates accepted BabyJubJub ballots, verifies five DLEQ decryption-share proofs, combines a 5-of-9 threshold without reconstructing the key, and publishes result hashes.

Honest boundary: threshold decryption is real off-chain cryptography. The trustee ceremony is deterministic for this demo and the on-chain tally SNARK remains pending.
