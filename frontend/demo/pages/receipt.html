Receipt page

Give the voter a private inclusion receipt.

The receipt proves a vote package was included in a batch manifest using a Merkle proof, without revealing the selected candidate.

Failure demonstration: a changed digest or sibling fails Merkle verification. A signed batcher intake deadline can also open a public omission claim; it remains unresolved until valid committed inclusion is shown. This is accountability, not a full liveness guarantee.
